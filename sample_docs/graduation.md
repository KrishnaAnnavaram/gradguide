# Applying to Graduate

## Graduation Application

Students apply for graduation through the student portal by the application deadline of the term in which they plan to finish. The deadline is the fourth Friday of the fall or spring term and the second Friday of the summer term. A non-refundable graduation fee of $75 is charged when the application is submitted.

If you do not finish in the term you applied for, you must reapply for a later term; the fee is charged again.

## Final Requirements Check

After applying, the graduate records office completes a degree audit against your approved degree plan. Any missing requirement is e-mailed to your university e-mail address within three weeks of the application deadline.

## Thesis Submission

Thesis students submit the final, committee-approved thesis to the graduate records office as a PDF no later than two weeks before the last class day. Formatting is reviewed against the thesis manual before the thesis is accepted.

## Commencement

Commencement ceremonies are held at the end of the fall and spring terms. Students who finish in the summer may walk in the following fall ceremony. Regalia can be ordered through the campus bookstore up to four weeks before the ceremony.

Participation in commencement is optional and does not affect the conferral of the degree.

## Diplomas

Diplomas are mailed to the diploma address on file about eight weeks after degrees are conferred. Update your diploma address in the student portal before the end of the term.
