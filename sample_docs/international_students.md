# International Graduate Students

These are general summaries. Immigration rules change, so always confirm your situation with the international student office before acting.

## Maintaining Status

Students on an F-1 visa must be enrolled full-time (9 credit hours) in each fall and spring term. Only one online course (3 credit hours) per term can count toward the full-time requirement.

A reduced course load is possible in limited cases, such as the final term or a documented medical condition, and must be approved by the international student office before the term's census date.

## Curricular Practical Training

Curricular Practical Training (CPT) allows paid or unpaid work that is an integral part of the degree, such as a required internship course. Students become eligible after completing one academic year in status. CPT must be authorized by the international student office before the work starts, and the student must enroll in the internship course for the term of the employment.

## Optional Practical Training

Optional Practical Training (OPT) allows up to 12 months of work related to the field of study after completing the degree. Students can apply as early as 90 days before the program end date and no later than 60 days after it. Work cannot begin before the approval notice arrives and the start date is reached.

Students in eligible science, technology, engineering or mathematics fields may apply for a 24-month extension before the initial OPT period ends.

## Travel Signatures

A travel signature on the I-20 is needed to re-enter the country after travelling abroad. Request it at least two weeks before departure. Signatures are valid for 12 months for enrolled students.
