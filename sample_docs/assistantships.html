Graduate Assistantships

Types of Assistantships

Teaching assistants support faculty with grading, labs and recitations. Research assistants work on a faculty member's funded research project. Both are paid positions of up to 20 hours per week.

Eligibility

  • Admission to a graduate degree program in good academic standing.
  • Full-time enrollment in every term of the appointment.
  • Teaching assistants whose first language is not English must pass the spoken English assessment before their first teaching appointment.

Tuition Benefits

Students appointed for at least 10 hours per week receive a tuition waiver covering up to 9 credit hours per term. Mandatory fees are not covered by the waiver.

How to Apply

Assistantships are offered by individual departments. Submit the assistantship application form to your department by March 1 for fall appointments and October 1 for spring appointments. Questions can be sent to the assistantship coordinator. Position descriptions are posted on the graduate jobs board.
