# Master's Degree Requirements

## Credit Hours

A master's degree requires a minimum of 30 graduate credit hours. At least 24 of those hours must be completed in residence, and no more than 6 hours may be transferred from another accredited institution.

Courses numbered below the graduate level do not count toward the degree, even when they are required as leveling coursework.

## Thesis and Non-Thesis Options

Students choose either the thesis option or the non-thesis option by the end of their second term of enrollment.

- Thesis option: 24 hours of coursework plus 6 hours of thesis credit, and an oral defense before a committee of three faculty members.
- Non-thesis option: 30 hours of coursework, including a capstone project or a comprehensive examination, depending on the program.

Switching options after the deadline requires approval from the program director.

## Degree Plan

Every student files a degree plan with their academic advisor before enrolling in their 12th credit hour. The degree plan lists the courses that will be applied to the degree. Changes to an approved degree plan are made with a degree plan change form signed by the advisor.

## Academic Standing

Graduate students must keep a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0. A student whose GPA falls below 3.0 is placed on academic probation for the next fall or spring term. Two consecutive terms on probation can lead to dismissal from the program.

A grade below C in a required course must be repeated. Only the most recent attempt counts toward the GPA.

## Time Limit

All requirements for a master's degree must be completed within six years of the first term of graduate enrollment. Courses older than six years at the time of graduation cannot be applied to the degree without a time-limit extension approved by the graduate dean.
