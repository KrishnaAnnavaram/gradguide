# Registration and Enrollment

## Full-Time Enrollment

Graduate students are full-time when enrolled in 9 or more credit hours in a fall or spring term, or 6 credit hours in the summer. Students holding a graduate assistantship must be enrolled full-time.

## Registration Holds

A hold on your account blocks registration until it is cleared. Common holds are:

- Advising hold: placed on all new students. Meet your academic advisor to have it removed.
- Financial hold: an unpaid balance of $200 or more. Pay the balance or set up a payment plan through the student billing portal.
- Immunization hold: upload the required immunization records through the health center portal.

Holds can take up to two business days to clear after the issue is resolved.

## Adding and Dropping Courses

Courses can be added online through the first week of the term. After the first week, adding a course requires approval from the course faculty member.

Courses dropped before the census date (the 12th class day of a fall or spring term) are removed from the transcript and fully refunded. Courses dropped after the census date receive a grade of W, and tuition is not refunded.

## Withdrawing From the Term

To withdraw from all courses, submit the term withdrawal form before the last day to withdraw, which is published in the academic calendar. Students on a student visa must speak with the international student office before withdrawing.

## Leave of Absence

Students who need to pause their studies for one or two terms can request a leave of absence. The request needs approval from the advisor and the program director, and the time on leave does not count toward the six-year time limit.
